Privacy

Privacy limits for the referral pages.

These pages are designed to measure page and CTA performance without collecting sensitive gambling or account data.

What may be measured

If privacy-safe analytics are configured, Rooted may measure page views, referral CTA clicks, FAQ opens, referrer/source/UTM values, and broad country-level traffic where the analytics provider supports it without identifying visitors.

What is not collected here

  • Names, account IDs, Duel usernames, deposits, withdrawals, wagers, balances, or gambling behaviour.
  • Payment details, wallet addresses, identity documents, or signup outcomes.
  • Data for retargeting minors, self-excluded people, or sensitive audiences.

Outbound links

The referral CTA opens https://duel.com/r/cromision and is labelled with rel="nofollow sponsored". Rooted does not cloak the destination.

Cookies and analytics

The current static implementation includes no third-party advertising scripts. The event hook is compatible with privacy-safe analytics such as Plausible or a first-party endpoint if configured later.

Contact

For privacy questions about Rooted pages, email hello@rooted.cloud.

Duel account and gambling data should never be sent to Rooted.
