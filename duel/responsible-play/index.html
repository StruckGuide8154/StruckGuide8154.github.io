Responsible play

Do not play if gambling causes harm.

Gambling is risky entertainment, not income, investing, or a way to solve financial pressure. You can lose money.

Do not continue if any of this applies

  • You are under the legal gambling age where you live.
  • You are in a restricted location or are unsure whether Duel is legal for you.
  • You are self-excluded, trying to stop, or have been told gambling is causing harm.
  • You feel pressure to win back losses, hide gambling, borrow money, or chase outcomes.

Practical limits

If you choose to gamble where legal, set hard deposit, time, and loss limits before you start. Stop when those limits are reached. Do not use credit, borrowed money, rent, bills, or emergency funds.

Support resources

  • GamCare
  • BeGambleAware
  • National Council on Problem Gambling
  • Gamblers Anonymous

Availability varies by country. Search for local gambling support services if these are not appropriate for your location.

Referral reminder

The referral link may credit the referrer. That is not a reason to gamble. Use it only if you were already planning to check Duel and only if the live terms and your local law allow access.
